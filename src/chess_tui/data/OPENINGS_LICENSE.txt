"Opening data is in a.tsv, b.tsv, c.tsv, d.tsv, and e.tsv, with the
filenames corresponding to ECO volumes."

Columns bundled here: eco, name, pgn
(epd and uci columns are computed at load time by chess_tui.openings.)

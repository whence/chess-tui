2. Chess opening catalog (src/chess_tui/data/{a,b,c,d,e}.tsv)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Source: https://github.com/lichess-org/chess-openings
License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication)
         https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The bundled TSV files are the upstream `a.tsv` through `e.tsv`,
distributed under CC0. As a collection of chess facts (ECO codes,
opening names, canonical PGN move sequences) the data is in the public
domain. Columns bundled: eco, name, pgn. The `uci` and `epd` columns
that the upstream `dist/` artifact includes are computed at load time
in `src/chess_tui/openings.py` to keep the bundled data identical to
the source repository.
